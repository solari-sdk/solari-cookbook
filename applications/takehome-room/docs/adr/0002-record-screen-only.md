# Record the screen only: no webcam, no audio

The Recording captures the Room's screen and nothing else. Software analysis of webcam video can bring in Illinois biometric law (BIPA). It can also bring in the Illinois AI Video Interview Act. Audio can bring in all-party consent rules. Screen-only capture avoids most of that and still shows how the Candidate worked. We still ask for consent in the invitation and keep a retention and deletion policy for GDPR. This is not legal advice. Adding webcam or audio later needs a fresh legal review, not just a code change.
